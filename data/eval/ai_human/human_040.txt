Parchment is a service that schools use for transcripts so they do n't have to mess with it themselves . Parchment charges enough to make it profitable for them , but if it 's too expensive , it would alienate the high schools they depend on . If they charged too much , another company would contact the school and make a better deal . ACT , on the other hand , makes its revenue two ways -- charging for tests , and charging for sending test scores . They are the only source for this information , and charge what the market will bear .
