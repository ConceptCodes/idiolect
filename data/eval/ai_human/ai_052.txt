White rice is often cheaper than brown rice because it is more processed and has had the bran and germ removed, which are the outer layers of the rice grain. These outer layers contain most of the nutrients, fiber, and flavor of the rice, so brown rice is generally considered to be more nutritious than white rice. However, the processing of white rice is simpler and requires fewer steps, which makes it cheaper to produce. The demand for white rice is also generally higher than that for brown rice, particularly in many developing countries where it is a staple food. This higher demand can also contribute to the lower price of white rice.
